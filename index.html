Corporate Profile

About Saira Enterprises

Saira Enterprises operates as an international trading house, textile exporter, corporate supplier, and technology group connecting Karachi, Pakistan and Casablanca, Morocco.

SAIRA ENTERPRISES

Our operational mandate focuses on tangible trade execution and practical digital engineering. In physical commodities, we supply commercial denim fabric rolls directly from Pakistani mills, export authentic hand-carved wooden handicrafts, and import high-utility household and kitchen goods from China.

Through our sister technology firm, SS Soft Automation Technologies, and our Moroccan import entity, WITATEX S.A.R.L, we provide end-to-end reliability across both physical supply chains and enterprise software systems.
