About Saira Enterprises

Founded in 2000 (with Moroccan company roots established in 1998), Saira Enterprises exhibits and exports Pakistani handmade carpets and wooden handicrafts across more than 24 countries worldwide while leading denim fabric trade and indenting in Morocco since 2020.

SAIRA ENTERPRISES

Global Trade · Premium Textiles · Intelligent Technology

Established in the year 2000, Saira Enterprises built its international reputation exhibiting and exporting authentic Pakistani handmade carpets and hand-carved Chiniot & Peshawar wooden handicrafts to more than 24 countries worldwide—alongside importing utility household and kitchen goods from China and fulfilling corporate gift mandates in Pakistan.

Having established our company in Morocco in 1998, we expanded our North African operations in 2020 to launch dedicated denim fabric trade and mill indenting in Casablanca through WITATEX S.A.R.L—complemented by enterprise software engineering from SS Soft Automation Technologies.
